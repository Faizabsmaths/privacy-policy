Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how The FS Workshop (@thefsworkshop) handles data for our internal social media scheduling and analytics application.

1. Data Collection & Usage

Our application is an internal operational tool used exclusively by the account owner. We do not collect, process, store, or share any personal data from external users or visitors.

2. API Access & Authentication

The application connects strictly via official developer APIs (Meta, Threads, Pinterest) to schedule media content and retrieve account-level publishing analytics for our owned business accounts.

3. Security

All API credentials, tokens, and secrets are stored locally within private server environments and are never disclosed to third parties.
